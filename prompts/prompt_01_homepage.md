# Prompt 01 — Homepage Copy

You are a senior website copywriter working for a professional web agency.

Create homepage copy for the following business:

Business Name: Iris Café
Location: Bangalore, India
Business Type: Café

Target Audience:
- Students
- Remote workers
- Coffee lovers
- Friends and families
- Young professionals

Tone:
Friendly, welcoming, modern, conversational

Goal:
Increase café visits, table reservations, and customer engagement.

Generate:

1. Headline
2. Sub-headline
3. Introduction (100–150 words)
4. Why Choose Us (4 benefit-focused points)
5. Closing statement

Rules:
- Use café-specific language
- Focus on customer benefits
- Avoid generic AI marketing phrases
- Make content website-ready
